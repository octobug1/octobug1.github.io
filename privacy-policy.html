Information is not sold. It may be shared with service providers who help operate communications, hosting, or agreed project work, or where required by law. This site links to third-party websites and services. Their privacy practices are separate from this policy, so review their policies before providing them with information.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the use of your personal information. To ask a privacy question or make a request, email octobug1@octobug.uk.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the website or services change. The effective date above shows when it was last revised.
